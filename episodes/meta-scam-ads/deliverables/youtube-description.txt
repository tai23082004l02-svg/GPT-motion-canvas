TITLE: Why Scam Ads Keep Finding You — Meta’s New Lawsuit Explained

The ad looks professional. The platform feels familiar. So why could it still be a scam?

On October 22, 2026, Rhode Island sued Meta over scam advertising. Meta disputes the allegations. This animated explainer separates the reported claims, Meta’s response, and what the numbers do — and don’t — tell us. Then we follow Alex through a fictional investment ad, private group and fake dashboard.

Alex, the presenter and every depicted ad/interface are original illustrations, not evidence from the case or a real victim’s experience. Legal status is as of October 23, 2026. Allegations are not findings. Meta’s ad-removal count and FTC social-media-loss data measure different things.
