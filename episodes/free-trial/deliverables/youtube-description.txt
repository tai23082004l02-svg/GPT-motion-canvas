TITLE: Why Your Free Trial Is So Hard to Cancel

Alex signs up for one documentary. A month later, a renewal charge sends him searching for the exit. Why can joining be so much easier than leaving?

Follow an animated cancellation maze, learn to identify who bills you, and separate canceling future renewals from asking for a refund. FRAME, Alex and every depicted interface are fictional examples. Policies vary by service; follow your provider’s official instructions.
