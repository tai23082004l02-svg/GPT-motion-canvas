# Sources, assumptions and calculation method

This episode follows one fictional driver, Alex. Dollar amounts are an illustrative US-dollar budget, not national averages, live rates, quotes, or a forecast.

## Consumer-finance references

- [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: Auto loans](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/auto-loans/) — comparing vehicle financing, affordability and loan terms.
- [CFPB: Difference between an interest rate and APR](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-the-difference-between-a-loan-interest-rate-and-the-apr-en-733/) — APR may include fees. The example assumes no loan fees, so the stated APR equals the nominal annual interest rate used in the payment formula.

These references support the explanation and comparison method. They are not the source of the hypothetical dollar estimates. No claim about the national average cost of owning a car is made. Reference pages checked during production on 23 October 2026.

## Exact example

| Input | Assumption |
|---|---:|
| Purchase price | $30,000 |
| Down payment | $3,000 |
| Principal financed | $27,000 |
| Nominal annual interest rate / APR with no loan fees | 7% |
| Loan term / ownership period | 60 months / 5 years |
| Sales tax, paid upfront and not financed | 6%, or $1,800 |
| Registration and related recurring fees | $250/year |
| Insurance, unchanged for the full period | $200/month |
| Distance | 1,200 miles/month |
| Fuel economy | 30 miles/gallon |
| Fuel price, unchanged average | $3.50/gallon |
| Maintenance, tires and repairs actually spent | $100/month on average |
| Assumed resale value after five years | $15,000 |

Parking, tolls, tickets, dealer add-ons, fees beyond the stated allowances and opportunity cost are excluded. Resale value is an assumption, not a forecast. Actual insurance, tax, financing, maintenance and fuel costs vary. This is a scenario, not a promise of a particular outcome.

## Method and results

Monthly loan payment = principal × monthly rate / (1 − (1 + monthly rate)^−60), where monthly rate = 0.07/12.

- Unrounded monthly loan payment: **$534.6323605894**.
- Total loan payments: **$32,077.9416353661**.
- Interest: **$5,077.9416353661**.
- Fuel: 1,200 ÷ 30 × $3.50 = **$140/month**, or **$8,400** across five years.
- Insurance: **$12,000** across five years.
- Maintenance and repairs: **$6,000** actually spent across five years.
- Registration: **$1,250** across five years.
- Cash paid out: $3,000 down + total loan payments + tax + insurance + fuel + maintenance + registration = **$64,527.9416353661**.
- Ownership cost: cash paid out minus $15,000 resale = **$49,527.9416353661**.
- Average ownership cost: **$825.4656939228/month**.
- Monthly cash budget excluding upfront cash: **$995.4656939228/month**. Individually rounded line items may differ from the rounded total by $1. The total uses unrounded inputs.

Equivalent check: $15,000 depreciation + interest + tax + insurance + fuel + maintenance + registration = the same ownership cost. Loan principal and depreciation are not added together.

The $20,000 comparison uses the same $3,000 down payment, 7% rate, no loan fees and 60-month term. Its $17,000 principal gives a monthly payment of about $337, approximately $198 less. This compares payments only; it does not claim equivalent insurance, maintenance or resale value.
