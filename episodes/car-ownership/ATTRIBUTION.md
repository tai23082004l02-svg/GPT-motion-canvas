# Asset and voice provenance

- Characters Alex and the mechanic, vehicles, scenery, props, thumbnail and animation: original procedural vector artwork created for this episode. No third-party stock footage or character art is used.
- Narration: generated locally with **Kokoro-82M**, voice **am_michael**, American English, speed **1.05**, through `kokoro-onnx`. This is a synthetic narrator; no claim of a human voice performance is made.
- [Kokoro-82M model](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M): Apache License 2.0, as listed in the model card. See the model repository for license and provenance details.
- [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx): inference implementation; see its repository for its license. Model and voices obtained from the public `model-files-v1.1` release. They are downloaded separately and not committed here.
- Background score, coin sounds, paper sounds, engine accents and transition sounds: original procedural synthesis in `sound_design.py`. No external music recordings or samples are used.
- Typography: DejaVu Sans, rendered with the system font. Font binaries are not bundled; see [DejaVu Fonts license](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html).
- Animation engine: [Motion Canvas](https://motioncanvas.io/), MIT license. Dependencies keep their respective licenses.
