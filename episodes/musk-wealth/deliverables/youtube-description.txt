How rich is Elon Musk, really? Start with a shopping cart, an iPhone 18 Pro, and a very unreasonable number: roughly 770 million phones.

Then comes the question that changes everything: how are you paying?

This illustrated explainer explores the difference between net worth and cash, how ownership gets valued, why selling changes the equation, and what extraordinary wealth can — and cannot — buy.

Figures are a dated snapshot, not a live wealth tracker. We use Forbes’ $922.9 billion estimate dated October 23, 2026, 9:30 p.m. EDT, and Apple’s $1,199 US starting price for iPhone 18 Pro, before sales tax. Phone counts are hypothetical wealth equivalents, not a feasible purchase. Other fictional examples and fixed assumptions are labeled in the video.
