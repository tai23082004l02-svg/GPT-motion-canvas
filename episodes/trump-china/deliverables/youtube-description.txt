What if Donald Trump had grown up in China—and the same ambition met a different rulebook?

This animated thought experiment follows a fictional counterpart through three possible paths: the builder, the showman, and the power seeker. Along the way, we ask what happens when family opportunities, history, media and political institutions change.

FICTION / SATIRE: These alternate-life scenes, meetings and reactions are invented. This is not documentary footage, a prediction, a real Trump quotation or a claim about how Chinese people would behave. Historical context is labeled separately. Constitutional comparisons describe the stated framework; rules and institutions evolved over time.
