TITLE: Why You Keep Scrolling When You’re Not Even Having Fun

You opened your phone for two minutes. Now it is past midnight, and the feed is barely entertaining. Why is the next swipe still so tempting?

Follow Alex through the checking loop, the missing stopping points, and a small experiment for making the next decision visible. This animated explainer draws on research about habit, smartphone checking, social reward learning, and interface design. The story and app interface are fictional; the practical suggestions are experiments, not guaranteed treatments.
