# Attribution and production

Original English script, fictional Joan/Will/Kit character rigs, vector props and diagrams, scene layouts, music composition and synthesized Foley for SCOPIVO. No stock music or stock sound-effect recordings. The reference video supplied by the user informed the broad illustrated-history format only; none of its frames, audio, characters or wording is redistributed.

Three background plates (village, hall and pantry) and the thumbnail were generated with OpenAI image generation. The working implementation uses image generation plus reusable vector layers; **ComfyUI was not installed or used**. Backgrounds are not photographs or verified archaeological reconstructions. Original PNG outputs were converted to WebP for browser delivery; the thumbnail was sized to 1280×720.

Narration: local Kokoro `am_michael` through [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx) (MIT); [Kokoro 82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) model (Apache 2.0). Voice/model files are not committed. Alignment uses faster-whisper, with caption wording restored to the canonical script. Music and Foley are generated by `production/sound_design.py`.

Motion Canvas: MIT. Browser export dependencies and notices remain under `viewer/public/licenses/`.

Text uses a Times New Roman font preference, with the bundled Liberation Serif fallback (SIL OFL, `viewer/public/fonts/Liberation-LICENSE.txt`). No Microsoft font files are distributed.

Research sources are linked and paraphrased in `sources-and-assumptions.md`; source articles, museum photos and book PDFs are not included in the published assets.
