No thermostat. No electric blanket. A very long winter.

How did people stay warm, cook, work, and sleep before electricity? Follow Joan, Will, and Kit through a winter day in southern England around 1400—from wool layers and an open hearth to stored food and a small pool of light.

This family and its dialogue are fictional. The episode is an illustrated historical reconstruction informed by museum interpretation and archaeological research. It explores one setting; people in other places, periods, and circumstances used different solutions. Comfort was unequal, and survival was never guaranteed.
