# Research and reconstruction notes

Checked 24 October 2026. Scope: a fictional farming household in southern England, around AD 1400. Joan, Will and Kit, their speech, the puddle, the household’s precise furnishings, and the order of chores are original narrative inventions. They are not people identified in a historical record. The opening title card and narration disclose this.

This is an illustrated explanation, not a measured reconstruction of one house. It does not cover every climate, century, social group, or pre-electric heating system. No mortality, indoor-temperature or required-fuel figures are invented.

## Deliberate visual conventions

- White round faces and stick limbs are a cartoon convention, not an assertion about skin colour or anatomy. No existing channel’s characters, images or script are copied.
- Snow depicts a frosty episode; narration explicitly includes rain, damp and mud. Southern England is not presented as continuously buried in deep snow.
- Three AI-generated background plates are illustrative, not documentary evidence. The exact structural geometry, vessels and clothing are simplified.
- Houses in the main story have open hearths; the fireplace and stove at the end are a comparison across technologies, not equipment belonging to this family.
- Night scenes are brightened for readability. The light circles and heat zones are qualitative.
- This is history entertainment, not instructions for using fires indoors. No claim of safe modern replication is made.
